Support Privacy

Effective 9 November 2026

Your journal.
Your devices.

Privacy policy for Kurukuru, its widgets and its Apple Watch app, developed by Neorgon.

Data Not Collected. Kurukuru has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own. Your journal stays on your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch. Neorgon never receives it.

Notifications and photos are optional, and files leave the app only when you export or share them. Each is explained below.

What stays on your devices

  • Your journal. Your wheel of hobbies, your check-ins with their moods, energy, flow, labels, what helped and notes, your seasons, your compass notes, the hobbies you mark as learning with their When or where lines, the steps you finish under Steps taken, levels, titles and discoveries. They are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone, which its widgets on the same iPhone read.
  • Your name and picture. The name Kurukuru greets you with, and the avatar photo if you choose one. Both stay on your iPhone.
  • Daily copies. The first save of each day leaves a copy on your iPhone, and the newest 14 are kept, along with a copy of anything an import replaced. They are never uploaded.
  • Preferences. Settings such as Sounds, Haptics, Motion, reminders and your hemisphere.
  • On Apple Watch. Only what the dial needs: your wheel's hobbies with their colours and levels, the current hobby, today's marks and your latest check-in, plus any mark you made on the watch that has not reached your iPhone yet. Your iPhone and your watch exchange this directly, through Apple's watch connection; it does not pass through Neorgon.

There is no Neorgon cloud and no account sync. Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups may include the app's data, according to your backup settings.

Optional permissions

Notifications

Kurukuru asks to send notifications only when you turn on reminders in Settings. Reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules for itself; nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Photos

An avatar comes from the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you pick and no access to the rest of your library. If you save a shared image to Photos, iOS asks first, and that permission lets the app add images, not read them.

Widgets, Live Activity, Control Center and Siri

These run on your iPhone and read the same local journal. The Live Activity is updated by the app on the device, with no push service.

Exports and sharing, only when you make them

Under You > Settings > Your data you can export a spreadsheet of your check-ins, a full app backup of everything Kurukuru keeps, or a website backup that opens on hobbyorbit.neorgon.com, with your notes only if you include them. Only the full app backup carries your learning marks and Steps taken. The file goes wherever you send it from the share sheet. Importing a backup reads the file you choose and changes nothing until you confirm.

Export insights, on the Cycles tab, saves every insight as a .json and a .csv file: the hobby it is about, its sentence and the counts behind it, with no notes. Those files, too, go only where you send them.

When you share an image of your orbit, it carries your hobby names, your initials or the photo you chose, the date and a code that links to hobbyorbit.neorgon.com: no notes, moods, levels or counts. A discovery shared from Cycles carries its sentence, its counts and the hobby it names. One of Kuru's postcards can be shared as an image only when it does not quote your notes. Each image goes only where you send it.

Websites and Apple

Links in the app open in your browser, and only when you tap one. No link carries anything from your journal. Opening a page on hobbyorbit.neorgon.com, these pages included, works like any website visit: the site's hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives the requested page, your IP address and browser details, and so does Cloudflare, which serves the shared Neorgon stylesheets from cdn.neorgon.org.

The Guide names the studies and books behind its chapters. Opening one takes you to doi.org, which forwards you to the publisher's site, or to PubMed, PubMed Central or Open Library, and each of those sites handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Hobby Orbit on the web keeps its own journal in your browser, separate from the app's; the two meet only through backup files you move yourself.

Apple handles App Store downloads, TestFlight and system diagnostics under its own privacy terms and your device settings. If you choose to share analytics or TestFlight feedback with developers, Apple may pass crash reports and that feedback to us. Kurukuru adds no analytics or crash-reporting code of its own.

Keeping and removing data

Your journal stays until you change it in the app or delete the app. A step you remove from Steps taken leaves that list for good, but its words stay in the app's storage, and so in its daily copies and in any full app backup you export afterwards, until you delete the app. Deleting Kurukuru from your iPhone removes its data there, daily copies included; removing the watch app removes what the watch kept. Copies in your device backups, and files you exported, are managed by you.

Neorgon cannot see, restore or delete your journal, because it never leaves your devices. Keep a backup if you want to move to a new iPhone.

Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them only to answer you and handle your request, never for advertising or sale. We keep the correspondence only as long as the request needs, and you can ask us to delete it. Please leave out journal entries unless they explain the problem and you want us to see them.

Children

Kurukuru is a general-audience app. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included. If a child's personal information has reached us in a support email, contact us and we will delete it.

Questions or changes

For privacy questions or requests, write to Neorgon at support@neorgon.org.

We will update this policy, and its effective date, when the app's data practices change. A feature that syncs through a server or adds an account would need a new policy before it ships.
