Kurukuru for iPhone and Apple Watch

Your hobbies,

Kurukuru (くるくる, "round and round") is Hobby Orbit as an iPhone app: a hobby wheel and a private journal for interests that come and go in cycles, with a dial for Apple Watch.

Coming to the App Store

What it does

  • Spin, then chooseA spin only suggests. A check-in records what you picked, how it felt, your energy and what helped.
  • Draw a cardInside a hobby, three ideas that belong with it. Swipe right to keep one, left to pass.
  • Learn your cyclesYour day as a clock, your week as a rhythm, your year as seasons, always with the counts behind them.
  • Come back without guiltLevels and titles, a rested glow for time away. No streaks, nothing decays and nothing expires.
  • On your wristTurn the Digital Crown to pick a hobby and tap to check in. Complications show today's marks.
  • Without opening itHome Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity, Control Center controls and Siri.

Common questions

Where does my journal live?

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. There is no account and nothing is uploaded: Neorgon never receives your journal. Your Apple Watch keeps only what its dial needs, your wheel and today's marks, plus any mark still waiting to reach your iPhone.

Each day the first save leaves a copy on your iPhone, and the newest 14 stay, under You > Settings > Your data. Your iCloud or computer backups of the iPhone may include the app's data, according to your own settings. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Can I move my journal between the website and the app?

Yes, as a file; nothing syncs on its own. In the app, You > Settings > Your data exports a Website backup that opens on hobbyorbit.neorgon.com (notes only if you include them), a Full app backup for another iPhone, or a Spreadsheet.

To bring your web journal into the app, use Download backup on the website, then Import your backup during setup or Choose a backup file under Your data. You see what the backup holds and the dates it covers first; nothing changes until you choose Replace, and the app keeps a copy of what it replaced.

How do I use the Apple Watch app?

It installs with the iPhone app when Automatic App Install is on; otherwise open the Watch app on your iPhone and install Kurukuru from Available Apps. Open Kurukuru on your iPhone once, with hobbies on the wheel, so it can send the wheel over.

On the watch, turn the Digital Crown to pick a hobby and tap the dial to check in, then choose how it was, or Later. A mark made away from your iPhone waits on the watch and reaches your journal when they meet again; one that arrives too late comes back as Not added, with Add it now or Drop. To show today's marks on a watch face, touch and hold the face, tap Edit and choose Kurukuru.

Can I turn the sounds off?

Yes. You > Settings > Sounds switches every cue on or off, and Hear them plays each one. They play alongside your music and stay quiet when your iPhone is silenced. With Sounds off, reminders arrive without a sound. The watch's check-in sound follows the same setting.

Will it send me reminders?

Only if you turn them on in Settings. Reminders are notifications your iPhone schedules for itself; nothing is sent from a server, and the evening suggestion comes only on days you already checked in.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The watch app needs an Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later, paired with that iPhone. The app is in English.

Contact

Email support@neorgon.org for help, a bug or a privacy request.

For a bug, include the app version from You > Settings, your iOS or watchOS version and the steps that led to it. Screenshots are optional. Your journal stays private: send an entry only if it explains the problem and you want us to see it.
