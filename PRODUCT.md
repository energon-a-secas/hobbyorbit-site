# Hobby Orbit: product one-pager

**What it is:** Explore your hobbies on a wheel and record moods, switches, and the things that draw you back

**Who it is for:** Someone whose interests shift over time and who wants to
externalize those shifts, describe how they feel, and notice what draws them back.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A person opens their phone during a quiet evening, uncertain what
they feel like doing, and wants a familiar wheel and one short place to write.

**What done looks like:** A hobby or rest has been chosen freely, a feeling has
been recorded if wanted, and the next visit can pick up from that record.

## Principles

- A spin suggests. Only a deliberate check-in records current activity.
- The wheel's planned order and the journal's observed sequence stay distinct.
- Not being in the mood and resting are valid states, with no streak penalties.
- Personal notes stay on this device; sharing shows an explicit preview.
- The user confirmed an installable web app as the first release.

## Interface

Use the existing Neorgon product design system and header/footer kits. Its dark
default suits the scene; user-selected fleet themes remain available. Coral is
the site accent, while hobby colors are editable data. Favor a large wheel,
plain labeled controls, short check-ins, keyboard access, 44px touch targets,
and reduced-motion support. Avoid productivity scores, invented history, and
claims that a short journal can predict or explain the user's behavior.
